// legal & privacy

The compliance starts here.

No cookie banner on this blog — because there is nothing to consent to. What the law does require is below: who publishes, who hosts, what is (not) collected, and your rights.

01 Publisher

SAM LABBES//ABB — independent developer · Vosges, France

Directeur de publication: Sam Labbe. Contact: sam@slabb.dev

blog.noirebox.eu is published as a personal, non-commercial blog showcasing open-source work.

02 Personal data — what this blog does NOT collect

  • Zero cookies — no consent banner is needed when there is nothing to consent to.
  • Zero tracking — no analytics, no ads, no social pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • Zero personal data processed by reading these posts.

03 Email — the only channel

If you write to us, your email address and message are used solely to answer you (legal basis: legitimate interest / pre-contractual steps). They are kept only as long as needed and are never sold, rented or shared.

04 Hosting

This blog is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), fronted by Cloudflare for DNS and protection. Hosting logs may be kept by these providers for security purposes, under their own policies and applicable safeguards.

05 Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability — write to us and it is handled without bureaucracy:

sam@slabb.dev

You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French supervisory authority.
